Common questions

Know what the search result means.

Can I find a county page by ZIP code?

Yes. SepticScope converts a representative ZIP coordinate to a likely county. Because ZIP areas can cross county lines, confirm the property’s actual county before relying on permit information.

Are local septic providers ranked or recommended?

No. Ordinary listings are neutrally ordered and based on documented public business and service-area information. Independently confirm current licensing, insurance, availability, scope, and pricing.

Does SepticScope issue septic permits?

No. SepticScope is independent and informational. The applicable county, health department, environmental health office, state program, or other authorized local agency controls permitting decisions.
